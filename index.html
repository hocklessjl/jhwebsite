One Team HUB: Digital Workplace Transformation

Replaced legacy shared drives with a governed, org-wide SharePoint intranet
+

Challenge

Fragmented storage across legacy G:, Z:, and Y: drives, inconsistent department ownership, and no centralized way for employees to find documents, forms, or resources.

Approach

  • Designed the full site architecture: homepage, department sites, requests & forms, resource lounge, groups, staff tickets, events.
  • Built a content governance model: lifecycle rules, department content-owner program, review/approval workflows, audits.
  • Ran a three-tier training program (foundational → content management → governance) plus lunch & learns, video walkthroughs, and an executive sponsorship track.

Impact

  • Migrated legacy departmental file shares to a governed, centralized platform, improving accessibility, security, and information management org-wide.
  • Standardized content ownership, reducing duplicate repositories and improving discoverability.
  • Strengthened cross-department communication and built a scalable foundation for future digital workplace initiatives.

Digital Student Referral System

Turned a paper/email intervention process into a tracked, automated pipeline
+

Challenge

Teachers relied on manual forms, emails, and verbal requests to flag students needing support: slow, disorganized, and hard to track over time.

Approach

  • Built a secure Google Form capturing referral reason, observed challenges, and requested interventions.
  • Piped submissions into Sheets for structured tracking, and used Autocrat to auto-generate formatted reports for the student support team.
  • Configured automatic notifications on submission, plus confirmation emails back to referring teachers.

Impact

  • Cut response time from days to hours via immediate notifications.
  • Eliminated paper-based referrals, tightening confidentiality and access control on student data.
  • Created a data-driven repository that surfaces recurring learning-challenge trends across student groups.

Student Pickup System Optimization

Rebuilt car-line logistics for a school district, cutting run time in half
+

Challenge

The original pickup process caused long wait times and street congestion, straining staff and frustrating parents.

Approach

  • Partnered with the carpool team to map every pain point in the existing process.
  • Designed and built a custom app: input carpool tag/description on one screen, automated student-name lookup and grouping on the caller-facing output screen.
  • Ran several testing rounds with staff, iterating on real feedback: separating input from output, supporting multi-student pickups.

Impact

  • Cut run time from 35 minutes to 15 minutes, a 20-minute reduction per pickup cycle.
  • Saved roughly $20,000 per year in reduced staff overtime and logistics management time.
  • Presented the build at the TCEA conference ("Sheets, Forms, and Formulas…Oh My!").

Google Workspace Audit

Turned a third-party security audit into a tracked remediation program
+

Challenge

A third-party audit surfaced a large, cross-cutting set of security, compliance, and optimization findings, many requiring coordination with people outside IT.

Approach

  • Categorized every finding into security, compliance (GDPR/COPPA/FERPA), user & group management, and optimization.
  • Assigned owners across IT, the business office, and department leads, with defined deadlines and auditable change logs.
  • Implemented one category at a time, security first, then tested and verified each change before moving on.

Impact

  • Reduced unauthorized-access risk through 2FA enforcement, sharing-setting cleanup, and tighter admin role controls.
  • Brought data retention and access practices into compliance with applicable regulations.
  • Delivered a documented audit trail that improved stakeholder trust and readiness for future audits.

Gmail Basics Training Course

Change-managed an org-wide Outlook → Gmail migration
+

Challenge

Users transitioning from Outlook faced real resistance: different mental models (labels vs. folders, conversation view) and mixed learning styles.

Approach

  • Identified the most common Outlook→Gmail friction points, then built step-by-step written guides plus screen-recorded video tutorials for each.
  • Produced a companion workbook with hands-on exercises, self-assessment, and bonus challenges for advanced users.
  • Delivered self-paced, backed by live Q&A and troubleshooting support.

Impact

  • 80%+ of participants reported feeling more comfortable with Gmail after training.
  • Fewer support tickets tied to email confusion and attachment issues.
  • Faster, more confident inbox organization using labels and Gmail-native features.

04 AI PHILOSOPHY

How I Use AI

Generative AI is a genuine productivity multiplier when it's pointed at the right problems. It doesn't replace judgment; it buys back the hours judgment actually needs.

Speed

It builds automations quickly: work that used to take days of workflow design can happen in minutes, from dashboards to project plans to full automation builds.

Signal

It catches errors that aren't easily seen by the naked eye, a second, tireless reviewer that scales in a way a human reviewer alone can't.

Range

It surfaces resolutions I might not have considered, expanding the option set on a problem rather than narrowing it to whatever I thought of first.

Leverage

Used right, it hands back man-hours, and that time goes to the highest-value work: evaluating and validating, not typing.

It's the same instinct behind every project on this page. Find the manual, repeatable, error-prone step, and remove it. Generative AI is just the newest, fastest tool for that job, and like any tool that touches real systems and real data, it earns trust the way the rest of my work does: tested, validated, and accountable to the people it affects.
